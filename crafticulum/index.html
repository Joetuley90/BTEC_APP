How it works

Three steps, and the first one you only do once.

  1. Add your subjectUpload the specification and Crafticulum reads the units and criteria. No specification? Type your topics in.
  2. Say what you're teachingOne sentence in your own words. The whole lesson comes back in about two minutes, and you can change any of it.
  3. Teach it and share itPut it on the board, download the PowerPoint, and give students a link or QR code.

Questions people ask

Who writes the lessons?

Crafticulum AI drafts each lesson against the criteria you picked. It is quick and usually right, but it can make mistakes, so read a lesson before you teach it. You can edit every part, swap any part for a new idea and run a fact check.

Which subjects and levels does it work with?

Any subject at any stage, from primary to university, in England, Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland. Upload the specification as a PDF and Crafticulum reads the units and criteria for you to check. If there isn't one, add your topics by hand.

What happens to learner data?

Students don't have accounts. Class lists can hold learner numbers and no names, and learner names are never sent to the AI. When work is marked with Second Marker, names and IDs are swapped out first and the work isn't kept.

Can we use our own lesson plan and slides?

Yes. Upload your lesson plan form and PowerPoint template once, and every download is filled in on them.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time from your Plan and billing page. You keep the plan until the end of the month you've paid for, then move to Free. Nothing you've made is deleted.

How do staff sign in?

With an email address and password, or with Google or Microsoft. A whole school or college can use its own single sign-on.
