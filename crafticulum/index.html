How it works

Four steps, and the first one you only do once.

  1. Add your subjectUpload the specification. Crafticulum reads the units and criteria, and you check what it found.
  2. Describe the lessonWrite it in your own words, or pick the criteria. A full lesson comes back in a couple of minutes, and you can edit all of it.
  3. Teach it and share itOpen the board in the classroom, give students the QR code, and download the documents you need.
  4. Mark the workIf your head of department has switched on Second Marker, upload a learner's work and it's checked against the same criteria, with names and IDs hidden first.

Questions people ask

Which subjects and levels does it work with?

Any subject at any stage: early years, primary, secondary, sixth form, college, apprenticeships, adult learning, university or professional training, in England, Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland. If there's a specification, upload it as a PDF and Crafticulum reads the units, learning aims and criteria from it for you to check before saving. If there isn't, add the subject and its topics by hand, so a lesson never needs an awarding organisation behind it.

Are the qualification details official?

Units and criteria come from the specification you upload, and you check them before they're used. The lists of stages, levels and awarding organisations are a starting set, not an official register, and each subject shows as not verified until it has been matched to Ofqual's register (for England and Northern Ireland) or someone on your staff has recorded their own check.

Can learners be kept anonymous?

Yes. A college's manager can switch on anonymous learners. Class lists then hold learner numbers, students handing in work give a number, and the server won't save a name.

What are AI Tools and Crafti?

AI Tools is a set of 32 tools for the everyday jobs around teaching: worksheets, quizzes, reports, emails, differentiated resources and leadership documents. Crafti is the assistant you can chat to. Both know your units and criteria, and everything they write is a draft for you to check and change. Your college's manager chooses who can use each part.

Who writes the lessons?

Each lesson is drafted by Crafticulum AI against the criteria you picked. You can edit every part by hand, swap any part for a new idea, and run a fact check on the teaching content before you use it.

Where is our data kept?

On your own web space. Lessons, planning, accounts and settings all stay on the server your college or school runs it on, with a daily backup.

Is learner work stored when it's marked?

No. Second Marker never keeps learner work on the server. Names, IDs, email addresses, phone numbers and postcodes are swapped out before the work is checked, and you make every marking decision yourself.

How do students get their lessons?

You share a link or a QR code. Students open the lesson page on a phone or a computer without an account, and each group can have a class page that collects everything you've shared with them.

How do staff sign in?

With an email address and password, or with Google, Microsoft or your college's own single sign-on. Two-step codes from an authenticator app can be switched on for any account.

Can we use our own lesson plan and slides?

Yes. Upload your lesson plan form and PowerPoint template once and every download is filled in on them.
