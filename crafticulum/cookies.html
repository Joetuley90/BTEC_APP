Cookies and what's stored on your device

Last updated 16 November 2026

This site uses one cookie, and it's needed to sign in. There are no advertising cookies and no tracking or analytics cookies, so there's no cookie banner to click through.

The cookie

NameWhat it doesHow long
ccsidKeeps a member of staff signed in. It holds a random number, not your name or email.Until you sign out, or 30 days after you last used the site unless the site's admin has set a different time
__stripe_mid, __stripe_sidSet by Stripe, only when someone opens the payment form to pay for a plan or change their card. Stripe uses them to spot fraud.Up to a year, and 30 minutes

Storage on your device

Your browser also keeps some things for this site in its own storage, so pages load faster and remember where you were. It stays on your device and isn't sent anywhere by being there.

WhoWhat's kept
StaffYour display choices, such as dark mode and the subject, level and group you last used. A copy of recent lessons and plans so the site opens quickly and works when the connection drops. Whether you've seen the quick start guide.
StudentsThe name you typed, so you don't have to type it again. Which tasks you've ticked off, your quiz scores, and which class page you opened.
EveryoneIf you install the site as an app, a copy of its pages so it opens without the internet.

You can clear all of it at any time in your browser's settings, under site data or browsing data. Staff will need to sign in again, and students will be asked for their name again.

Other companies

  • Google or Microsoft sign-in. If a member of staff chooses to sign in with Google or Microsoft, that company's sign-in page sets its own cookies, under its own policy.
  • Fonts and code libraries. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from cdnjs. They don't set cookies for this site, but like any web request they see your device's internet address.
