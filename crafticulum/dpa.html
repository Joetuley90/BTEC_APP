Data processing agreement

Last updated 20 November 2026

This agreement is between a school, college or training provider that uses Crafticulum ("the Institution") and Next Level Labs Ltd, trading as Crafticulum ("Crafticulum"), company number 16390028, registered office 4th Floor Office, 205 Regent Street, London, W1B 4HB. It applies from the day the Institution starts using Crafticulum and lasts for as long as Crafticulum holds the Institution's data. It sits alongside the terms of use and the privacy notice. If an Institution needs a signed copy, ask through the contact page.

1. Roles

The Institution is the controller of the personal data its staff put into Crafticulum. Crafticulum is the processor and handles that data only to provide the service.

2. What is processed

Whose dataStaff of the Institution. Learners, only where staff add class lists, marks, hand-in links or quiz and class pages.
Staff dataName, email address, role, department, sign-in details, profile details they choose to add, the lessons and plans they make, and a log of their activity.
Learner dataNames or learner numbers on class lists, marks against criteria, dates of hand-in and feedback, files handed in through a link (kept 30 days), and names typed into quiz or class pages.
Special category dataNone is asked for. Learner needs are recorded for a whole group, never against a named learner.
WhyTo let staff plan, build, share and mark, and to let leaders see their department's work.
How longFor as long as the Institution uses Crafticulum, then as set out in section 11.

3. Instructions

Crafticulum processes the data only on the Institution's written instructions, which are this agreement and the settings the Institution's staff choose in the service. Crafticulum will say so if it thinks an instruction breaks data protection law.

4. Learner data and AI

Learner names, marks and answers are not sent to the AI provider. Staff are told not to type learner names into lesson descriptions or upload documents that contain them. The Institution can switch on anonymous learners, which stops the service saving any learner name.

5. Confidentiality

Anyone at Crafticulum who can see the data is bound to keep it confidential.

6. Security

Crafticulum keeps the data secure with, at least: encryption in transit (HTTPS), passwords stored as one-way hashes, optional two-step sign-in, roles that limit who sees what, a log of changes, and daily backups. Crafticulum staff look at an Institution's data only to fix a problem the Institution has reported or to keep the service running.

7. Sub-processors

The Institution agrees to the sub-processors listed below. Crafticulum has a contract with each that protects the data and remains responsible for what they do. Crafticulum will give 30 days' notice, on this page and by email to the Institution's manager, before adding or replacing one. If the Institution objects on reasonable grounds and it can't be resolved, the Institution may end its plan and be refunded any unused period it has paid for.

8. Where data is kept

The live service is kept on servers in London, in the United Kingdom. Where a sub-processor processes data outside the UK, Crafticulum makes sure a transfer mechanism the law allows is in place.

9. Helping the Institution

Crafticulum will help the Institution, as far as it reasonably can, to answer requests from people about their data, to carry out data protection impact assessments, and to deal with the regulator. The Institution's manager can export or delete staff and learner records in the service at any time. If someone sends a request about their data straight to Crafticulum, Crafticulum passes it to the Institution and does not answer it itself.

10. Personal data breaches

Crafticulum will tell the Institution without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware, of any personal data breach affecting the Institution's data. It will say what happened, what data is involved and what it is doing about it, and will keep the Institution updated.

11. When the agreement ends

The Institution can download its lessons, plans and records before it leaves. Within 30 days of the end, Crafticulum deletes the Institution's data from the live service. Copies in backups are overwritten within a further 28 days. Crafticulum keeps data longer only where the law requires it.

12. Checks

Crafticulum will give the Institution the information it needs to show this agreement is being followed, and will answer a reasonable security questionnaire once a year. An on-site audit is by agreement, with reasonable notice, and no more than once a year unless there has been a breach.

13. Liability and law

Liability under this agreement follows the limits in the terms of use, except where the law does not allow a limit. This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
