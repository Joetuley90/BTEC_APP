Security at a glance

Last updated 20 November 2026 · Print or save as a PDF

One page for IT, data protection and safeguarding leads. The detail is in the data processing agreement and the privacy notice.

Who runs itNext Level Labs Ltd, trading as Crafticulum. Company number 16390028, registered in England and Wales. Registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, number Z92242123.
Where data is keptOn servers in London, in the United Kingdom, hosted by Krystal Hosting. The backups are kept in London too.
Your role and oursYour institution is the data controller. Crafticulum is the processor and only uses the data to provide the service.
Learner dataLearners have no accounts. A teacher can keep a class list and marks. Learner names, marks and answers are never sent to the AI. An institution can switch on anonymous learners, and then no learner name is stored at all.
What goes to the AIThe lesson description, the criteria and any specification or brief a member of staff uploads. It is sent to Anthropic to write the lesson. Staff are told not to include personal details.
Signing inGoogle, Microsoft or your own single sign-on (OpenID Connect), or a password with optional two-step sign-in. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Too many wrong attempts lock sign-in for a while.
Who can see whatStaff see their own department. Heads of department and managers see more, by role. New accounts are approved by a manager. A log records who changed what.
In transitEverything is sent over HTTPS. Sign-in cookies are marked secure and can't be read by scripts.
BackupsMade every day and kept for 28 days, with a remote copy in case the server is lost.
PaymentsCard payments are taken by Stripe. Card details go straight to Stripe and never touch Crafticulum. Institutions can pay by invoice instead.
Cookies and trackingOne cookie to keep you signed in, and one more only if you arrive from a colleague's invitation link. No advertising, analytics or tracking.
If something goes wrongWe tell an affected institution within 48 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach, with what happened and what we're doing about it.
LeavingYou can download your lessons and records at any time. When you leave, your data is deleted from the live service within 30 days and from the backups within a further 28.
Other companies involvedKrystal Hosting, Amazon Web Services, Google, Anthropic and Stripe. What each one gets is listed in the data processing agreement.
