Accessibility statement

Last checked 21 November 2026

We want everyone who teaches or learns with Crafticulum to be able to use it, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, magnification or voice control. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What you can do

  • Use the whole app with a keyboard. Every button and menu can be reached with Tab and used with Enter or Space, and Escape closes menus and pop-ups.
  • Zoom in to 400% without losing content. The app reflows to a single column on a small screen.
  • Use a screen reader. Buttons, menus, tabs and form fields have names, and messages such as "Saved" are announced.
  • Switch to dark mode, and change the text size, colours and spacing of the classroom board under Display.
  • Watch video lessons with captions, and have them read aloud.
  • Make a version of any lesson for learners with dyslexia, low literacy or English as an additional language.

How we checked

We test the public pages and the main parts of the app (the start page, a lesson, planning, the library, settings and the classroom board) with our own automated checks for colour contrast, names for buttons and links, labels for form fields, heading order, duplicate page elements and the size of things you tap. We also check by hand with a keyboard and at different zoom levels and screen sizes. This is our own testing, not an independent audit.

What we know isn't perfect yet

  • What Crafticulum AI writes. Lessons, quizzes and slides are written by AI. We ask it for clear headings and plain English, but you should check anything you share, especially for learners with particular needs.
  • Downloads. PowerPoint and Word files use real headings and list styles. Pictures you add to slides need alternative text adding in PowerPoint. If you upload your own template, its accessibility depends on the template.
  • Video lessons. They have captions, but no audio description of what is on screen. Everything on screen is also on the lesson page as text.
  • Charts in Admin. The business charts show their figures when you hover or focus on them, and the same figures can be downloaded as a spreadsheet.
  • Paying by card. The payment form is Stripe's. Stripe publishes its own accessibility information.

Tell us about a problem

If something doesn't work for you, or you need information in a different format, use the contact page or Report a problem inside the app. Tell us the page and what happened. We reply within five working days, and we fix accessibility problems ahead of other work.

For schools and colleges

If you need details from us for your own accessibility statement or procurement, get in touch and we'll send what you need.
